# Where to look for written campaign cases

For open searches about how the work was done, start with one or two programs that fit the topic and market. These editions have confirmed written cases in Awardy as of 21 November 2026.

| Program | Awardy ID | Edition | Case material |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The One Show | `the-one-show-global` | 2026 | Detailed global creative cases, many with video. |
| The Drum Awards | `the-drum-awards-global` | 2025 | Global marketing cases across industries and channels, many with video. |
| Golden Drum | `golden-drum` | 2025–2026 | Short regional creative summaries, usually with video. |
| Effie Europe | `effie-europe` | 2025 | Short European effectiveness summaries and images. |
| IAB MIXX Türkiye | `iab-mixx-tr` | 2026 | Detailed Turkish digital marketing cases, usually with video. |
| Effie Türkiye | `effie-tr` | 2025 | Detailed Turkish effectiveness cases for some winners. Video links across this edition. |

Check individual cases for write-ups and films. Follow the user's named program or edition if given, and check live coverage when newer results matter.
