# Awardy skills

Find award-winning marketing campaigns and see what the teams did. These skills give your AI assistant a way to search Awardy, handle short briefs, and follow up with case details.

| Skill | Use it to | Download |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Awardy campaign research](skills/awardy-campaign-research/) | Find winners by topic, industry, competitor, channel, award program, or category. Open the cases you want to explore. | [ZIP](https://github.com/mrcglyn/awardy-skills/releases/latest/download/awardy-campaign-research.zip) |
| [Connect Awardy](skills/connect-awardy-mcp/) | Set up Awardy in ChatGPT, Codex, Claude Code, or another assistant with MCP support. | [ZIP](https://github.com/mrcglyn/awardy-skills/releases/latest/download/connect-awardy-mcp.zip) |

## Get started

1. [Connect Awardy](skills/connect-awardy-mcp/) in the assistant you use.
2. [Install Awardy campaign research](skills/awardy-campaign-research/#install-the-skill).
3. Try asking “Find standout creator-led campaigns that drove product trial.” In ChatGPT, select **@Awardy** first.

The skills contain instructions and examples. Search results and case material come from your connected [Awardy account](https://awardy.ai/), subject to the coverage and access of your workspace.
